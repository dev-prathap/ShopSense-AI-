# AI Sales Agent for Shopify
## GTM One-Pager (MVP)

## 1. GTM Objective
Acquire and convert first cohort of paying Shopify merchants by proving measurable revenue impact within trial period.

## 2. Core Message
**Primary message:**
"Install AI Sales Agent and convert more visitors into buyers with 24/7 product recommendations, instant answers, and cart recovery prompts."

**Proof angle:**
- AI-attributed revenue dashboard
- Reduced repetitive support load
- Faster customer purchase decisions

## 3. Channel Strategy
### Channel A: Founder-led outbound
- Targets: Shopify founders, ecommerce managers, DTC operators
- Platforms: LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Shopify communities, partner agency intros

### Channel B: Content-led inbound
- Publish short-form conversion case notes and playbooks:
  - "How we recovered abandoned carts with AI prompts"
  - "Top 10 pre-sale questions AI can answer instantly"

### Channel C: Partner distribution
- Shopify agencies and consultants as referral channel
- Offer co-branded pilot onboarding package

## 4. Offer Structure
- 14-day free trial
- White-glove onboarding for first 20 stores
- Optional pilot setup call and weekly ROI review

## 5. Outreach Scripts
### Script 1: Cold DM (Founder)
"Hi <Name>, we built a Shopify AI sales agent focused on conversions, not just support replies. It recommends products, handles pre-sale questions, and tracks AI-attributed revenue. Open to a 10-minute walkthrough for your store?"

### Script 2: Follow-up (No response)
"Quick follow-up, we are onboarding a small pilot group this month. If conversion lift is visible in 14 days, continue; otherwise, cancel. Want the demo link?"

### Script 3: Trial-to-paid conversion prompt
"Your store saw <metric> AI-assisted conversations and <revenue> attributed revenue in trial. Upgrading keeps the assistant live and unlocks deeper analytics."

## 6. Trial-to-Paid Playbook
1. Day 0: Assisted install and baseline metric capture.
2. Day 2: Verify product recommendation relevance and adjust prompts/policies.
3. Day 5: Share early conversation and click data.
4. Day 10: Present attributed conversion snapshot.
5. Day 13: Upgrade call with ROI summary and plan recommendation.

## 7. Funnel Targets (Initial)
- 100 qualified outreach touches/week
- 20 demo conversations/week
- 8 trial starts/week
- 3 paid conversions/week

## 8. Feedback Loop into Product
- Track top objections from calls and chat logs.
- Feed gaps into weekly product sprint.
- Publish proof stories from successful pilot stores.
