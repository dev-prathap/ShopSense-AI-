# AI Sales Agent for Shopify
## Product Vision Document

## 1. Vision Statement
AI Sales Agent is a conversion-focused AI layer for Shopify storefronts that turns high-intent visitors into buyers through real-time, sales-grade conversations.

**Mission:** Increase merchant revenue by replacing passive browsing and static support interactions with guided, conversational commerce.

**12-month vision:** Become the default AI sales surface for SMB and mid-market Shopify brands by proving measurable lift in conversion rate, assisted revenue, and support deflection.

## 2. Market Problem
Shopify merchants consistently face:
- Low conversion rates (most visitors leave without buying)
- Slow decision-making due to unanswered product questions
- Poor product discovery across large catalogs
- High cart abandonment at checkout intent stage
- Support overload from repetitive pre-sale and order-status queries

Existing chat/support tools are optimized for ticket reduction, not revenue generation. Merchants need an AI agent optimized for **buying decisions** and **AOV uplift**.

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
Primary ICP for v1:
- English-speaking DTC Shopify brands
- 50 to 5,000 SKUs
- Small support team
- Founder-led or lean growth team
- Willing to test conversion tools with direct ROI visibility

Priority verticals:
- Fashion & accessories
- Beauty/personal care
- Consumer electronics/accessories
- Niche lifestyle DTC

## 4. Value Proposition
### Core outcome
Install one app and deploy a storefront AI sales assistant that:
- Recommends relevant products from live catalog context
- Answers buying-blocker questions instantly
- Handles order-status requests safely
- Nudges uncertain buyers with contextual recovery offers
- Proves value via attributable revenue analytics

### Merchant promise
- Faster buyer decisions
- Higher conversion and AOV
- Lower repetitive support burden
- Clear revenue attribution to AI interactions

## 5. Positioning and Differentiation
**Positioning statement:**
For Shopify brands that need more sales without expanding support headcount, AI Sales Agent is a revenue-first AI sales assistant that guides customers to purchase, unlike general chat widgets focused only on support automation.

Differentiators:
- Revenue-first conversational policy (not generic Q&A)
- Catalog-aware recommendation behavior with intent routing
- Built-in order tracking and cart-recovery paths in same conversational flow
- Merchant-friendly analytics around assisted conversion and attributed revenue

## 6. Pricing Hypothesis (v1)
- 14-day free trial
- Starter: $49/month
- Growth: $79/month
- Pro: $129/month
- Enterprise: custom

Early-stage pricing strategy:
- Optimize for rapid paid activation and testimonial velocity
- Keep plan differentiation simple (usage caps and analytics depth)
- Validate willingness to pay from conversion lift, not feature count

## 7. Product Scope Boundaries
### In scope for v1
- Shopify app install and onboarding
- Catalog sync and AI-assisted product discovery
- Product Q&A and recommendations
- Order tracking responses
- Cart recovery conversational offers
- AI analytics dashboard
- Trial and tiered billing baseline

### Deferred post-v1
- Voice shopping assistant
- WhatsApp and outbound channel automation
- Multi-model provider routing
- Deep lifecycle marketing automation (email/SMS orchestration)
- Advanced experimentation engine and multi-touch attribution

## 8. Success Metrics (Business)
Primary:
- Paid store activations
- Trial-to-paid conversion rate
- Net AI-attributed revenue per store

Secondary:
- Assisted conversion rate
- Recovery-offer acceptance rate
- Support deflection on repetitive queries
- 30-day store retention

## 9. 12-Month Product Vision Milestones
- Q1: Stable Shopify MVP with measurable assisted revenue
- Q2: Improve recommendation quality and analytics granularity
- Q3: Expand sales channels and campaign hooks
- Q4: Evolve into AI commerce operating layer across multiple workflows and channels
